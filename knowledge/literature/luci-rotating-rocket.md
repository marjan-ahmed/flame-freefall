## Scope

The presentation reports material flammability testing on a spinning vehicle
that simulates lunar gravity with centrifugal force. Its reported outcome is
that material flammability testing at partial-g is feasible using a spinning
vehicle.[^luci-2025]

## Reported observations

For the partial-g SIBAL fabric burn, the presentation states: "O2 concentration
decreases from ~20% to ~18%." For PMMA it states: "Flame spread rate increases
linearly from 0 to ~.6 mm/s (before gravity starts dropping at t = 125s)".
The instantaneous spread rate is described as a plotted derivative of a fit
equation.[^luci-2025]
