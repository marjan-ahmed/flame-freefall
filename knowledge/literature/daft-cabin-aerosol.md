## Scope

The presentation describes cabin aerosol sampling with a P-Trak and Dust Trak.
It reports sampling undisturbed air at locations in the lab and node, and a
test that deliberately generated aerosol by separating Velcro.[^daft-2007]

## Reported bounds

The campaign results report average cabin-air values less than 0.005 mg/m3
for Dust Trak and 15 particles /cm3 for P-Trak. These are reported bounds on
campaign averages. They are not individual combustion outcomes or established
instrument detection limits.[^daft-2007]
