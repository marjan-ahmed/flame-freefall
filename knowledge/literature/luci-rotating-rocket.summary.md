The LUCI presentation reports flammability observations from a rotating vehicle simulating lunar gravity. The SIBAL observation describes oxygen decreasing from approximately 20% to 18%. The reported PMMA spread rate increases from zero to approximately 0.6 mm/s before gravity begins dropping at 125 seconds; this rate is a derivative of a fitted position curve.

These publication observations are separate from the individual PSI test index. Independently identified records, uncertainty estimates and time-resolved source data remain open questions.
