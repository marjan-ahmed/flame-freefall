LUCI demonstrated partial-gravity flammability testing on a spinning vehicle.
The presentation reports 11 RPM and a 1.22 m effective radius, with a fabric
sheet and acrylic rod observed using orthogonal cameras and chamber sensors.

The source distinguishes char front, flame base and flame tip. SIBAL tracking
includes approximately steady spread; PMMA base motion is non-steady, and its
instantaneous spread rate is derived from a fitted position equation. The
ground comparisons report fitted rates rather than independent replicate
measurements.

Oxygen changes during burning, Coriolis deflection is visible, and the PMMA
slides contain differing descriptions of late gravity behavior. Source values,
printed ratios and ambiguities remain explicit. Tracking definitions,
synchronized diagnostic histories and uncertainties are needed before using
the observations for condition-matched comparisons or model validation.
