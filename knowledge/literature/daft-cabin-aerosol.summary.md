The DAFT presentation describes cabin aerosol sampling using P-Trak and Dust Trak instruments, including undisturbed cabin air and deliberately generated aerosol from separating Velcro.

Reported average cabin-air values are less than 0.005 mg/m3 for Dust Trak and 15 particles/cm3 for P-Trak. These are bounds on campaign averages, not individual combustion outcomes or established instrument detection limits. Identified sampling records, calibration and uncertainty remain open questions.
