NASA-STD-6001B is the certification standard for spacecraft material
flammability, offgassing, and fluid compatibility — the standard the
investigation reports in this corpus test against. Contains 425 numbered
"shall" requirements; this entry covers all of §4 (Evaluation and Testing),
§5 (Applicable Material Tests), §6 (System Evaluations), and §7 (all 18 Test
Methods and their pass/fail criteria).

- **Required tests by environment** (Table 1): habitable compartments need
  Tests 1, 4, 7, 18; LOX/GOX needs Tests 1, 17; breathing gases need Tests 1,
  7; reactive fluids need Test 15.
- **Test 1 / Test 4** (upward flame propagation / wire insulation): pass = no
  specimen burns >15 cm (6 in) and no burning-debris flame transfer, across 5
  specimens.
- **Test 7** (offgassing): pass = Toxic Hazard Index T < 0.5, calculated
  against habitable volume (ISS Destiny module = 118 m³, Shuttle = 65 m³,
  Orion = 15 m³).
- **Test 15** (reactive-fluid compatibility — hydrazine family, N₂O₄,
  ammonia): 2-hr screening + 48-hr immersion at 71±3°C (fuels) or 30±3°C
  (ammonia); fails on >20% dimension/property change or, for N₂O₄, a pressure
  rise >68.9 kPa.
- **Test 17** (GOX flammability): fails if any of ≥10 specimens burns >3 cm.
- **Test 18** (arc tracking): pass = ≥64 of tested wires pass dielectric test,
  ≤3 failures per bundle, ≤7.6 cm damage per bundle.
- Several older test numbers (2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13A/13B, 14, 16)
  are deleted or demoted to supplemental status, detailed in Appendix A.
- Not yet covered: Appendices A (full supplemental-test procedures), B
  (facility certification), C, D (submission forms), E (compliance matrix) —
  a comparable volume of additional requirements to everything above.
