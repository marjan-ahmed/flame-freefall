# Decadal survey

* [Thriving in Space (2023 Decadal Survey) — Combustion and Fire Safety](thriving-in-space.md) - The combustion-science and spacecraft fire-safety sections of the National Academies' 2023-2032 decadal survey for biological and physical sciences research in space.
* [Spacecraft Materials Fire Safety](miller-spacecraft-materials-fire-safety.md) - Decadal Survey topical white paper by Fletcher Miller et al. on how NASA's ground-based flammability certification may not be conservative for microgravity and partial-gravity conditions.
* [Challenges and Research Needs for Micro- and Partial-Gravity Fires](liao-micro-partial-gravity-fires.md) - Decadal Survey topical white paper by Ya-Ting Liao et al. on fire dynamics research needs for microgravity and partial-gravity spaceflight, including a direct call for a comprehensive fire database.
