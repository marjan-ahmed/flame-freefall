ACME s-Flame burned spherical diffusion flames around a porous spherical
burner in quiescent microgravity, in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack:
nitrogen dilution from Sept. 2019 to Jan. 2020, helium dilution from May to
July 2020. Analysis was still ongoing when the report was written.

- **Fuels:** hydrogen, methane, ethylene and H2/CH4 mixtures, diluted with
  nitrogen or helium, in air and enriched O2 environments.
- **Extinction:** nitrogen-diluted methane and ethylene flames seem to show
  kinetic extinction at low flow rates and radiative extinction at high flow
  rates; radiative extinction turned out to be common in ISS testing.
- **Soot:** ethylene flames did not soot in microgravity except briefly at
  ignition, so the effect of soot on extinction could not be assessed.
- **Helium:** it shifted extinction limits and severely affected ignition;
  the pulsating instabilities it was meant to reveal were not conclusively
  observed.
- Flame luminosity does not necessarily correspond with flame strength.
