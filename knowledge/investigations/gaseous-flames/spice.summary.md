SPICE measured laminar smoke points (the flame length at which a flame starts
to emit soot) of non-buoyant gas-jet flames in co-flowing air, in the ISS
Microgravity Science Glovebox, in twelve sessions from February 14, 2009 to
June 23, 2009.

- **Fuels:** ethylene, propane, propylene and propylene/nitrogen mixtures;
  methane smoke points could not be obtained.
- **Conditions:** burner diameters 0.41, 0.76 and 1.6 mm; co-flow velocities
  5.4 - 65 cm/s; ISS cabin air at 19 - 24% O₂; 526 flames ignited.
- **Smoke-point lengths by fuel:** C2H4 37 - 105 mm, C3H8 46 - 105 mm, C3H6
  13 - 59 mm, 75% C3H6 15 - 44 mm, 50% C3H6 22 - 50 mm.
- **Findings:** faster co-flow and smaller burners both lengthen the smoke
  point; smoke point lengths scale with d -0.91 uair0.41; sooting propensity
  runs ethylene < propane < 50% propylene < 75% propylene < propylene.
- **Caveat:** the report says both 52 and 55 smoke points; its per-fuel
  table sums to 52.
- Gaseous-flame geometry class: compare with ACME, not with solid-fuel or
  droplet tests.
