# Gaseous flames

* [ACME: Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments](acme.md) - Six gaseous-fuel laminar flame experiments on the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack, summarized from NASA's own poster presentation of selected results.
* [ACME BRE: Burning Rate Emulator](acme-bre.md) - Gas-fed porous burners that emulate burning condensed materials in quiescent microgravity, tested on the ISS in 2019-2021 in the exploration atmospheres NASA proposed, from the ACME/BRE final report.
* [ACME s-Flame: Structure and Response of Spherical Diffusion Flames](acme-s-flame.md) - Spherical diffusion flames from a porous spherical burner in quiescent microgravity, tested on the ISS in 2019-2020 with nitrogen and helium dilution, from the ACME/s-Flame final report.
* [SPICE: Smoke Point in Co-flow Experiment](spice.md) - Laminar smoke points of non-buoyant gas-jet diffusion flames in co-flowing air, measured in the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox in 2009, from the SPICE final report.
