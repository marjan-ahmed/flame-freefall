CFI followed up FLEX and FLEX-2 to study droplet **cool flames**: flames
driven by low-temperature chemistry at 700 to 900 K, without a visible flame.
It ran in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack from January 2017 through
September 2017. Its data was still to be fully analyzed.

- **Tests:** over two hundred, with droplets around 4 mm.
- **Fuels and pressures:** pure n-dodecane at 0.5 to 5 atm; n-dodecane/
  iso-dodecane 75/25% and 60/40% from 0.5 to 5 atm; farnesane from 0.75 to 3
  atm; helium dilution by 29% and 59%.
- **Findings (preliminary):** cool flames observed; the branched
  iso-dodecane reduces the tendency to form them; above 2 atm, multiple
  reignition cycles occurred.
- **Data map:** n-dodecane FLEX-1206 to FLEX-1284, mixtures FLEX-1285 to
  FLEX-1387, farnesane FLEX-1388 to FLEX-1431.
- **Caveat:** the findings write "n-dodecanol" where the rest of the report
  says n-dodecane.
