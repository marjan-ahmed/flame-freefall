# Droplet combustion

* [FLEX: Flame Extinguishment Experiment](flex.md) - Droplet-combustion tests aboard the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack — full apparatus, procedure, and the complete 275-test results table from March 2009 to December 2011.
* [FLEX-2: Fundamental Studies in Droplet Combustion and Flame Extinguishment](flex-2.md) - The planned objectives and test-matrix envelope of the FLEX-2 droplet experiments on the ISS, from the FLEX-2 Science Requirements Document (March 2010) — a plan, not a results report.
* [CFI: Cool Flame Investigation](cfi.md) - Droplet cool-flame experiments on n-dodecane, n-dodecane/iso-dodecane mixtures and farnesane in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack in 2017, from the CFI science final report.
* [FLEX-2 as-run cool-flame results (FLEX-team literature)](flex-2-cool-flame-results.md) - As-run FLEX-2 droplet-burn measurements reported by the FLEX team (n-decane, n-octane, n-heptane; hot- and cool-flame burning rates, extinction events) — literature evidence that supplements the planned-envelope PSI-68 table, whose rows carry no results.
