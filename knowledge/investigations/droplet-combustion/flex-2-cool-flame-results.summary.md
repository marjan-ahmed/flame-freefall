The FLEX-team literature reports hot- and cool-flame burning rates and stage events for n-decane and n-octane droplets, with qualitative heptane cases. The cool-flame stage is observed radiometrically after the visible flame extinguishes.

The PSI-68 rows are planned fuel envelopes. Literature burn identifiers are attached by exact fuel identity only and are not independent PSI row identities. The printed R383 atmosphere is internally inconsistent and remains as printed; the missing chronology, anonymous heptane cases and image-only later results remain limitations.
