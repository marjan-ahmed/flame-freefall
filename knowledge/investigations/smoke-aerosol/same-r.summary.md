SAME-R re-flew the SAME smoke experiment from July 16, to September 9, 2010,
completing 66 test points, after SAME's TEM grids came back contaminated.

- **What changed:** Pyrell replaced the DBP samples; higher heating
  temperatures and no-flow runs were added; the new M-pass instrument
  replaced one diagnostic enclosure.
- **Better data reduction:** tests are characterized by a derived sample
  heating temperature instead of Rt/R0, because the same Rt/R0 can differ by
  as much as 30 degrees Celsius.
- **Conditions (appended paper):** baseline air flow 8 cm/s, runs down to 2
  cm/s, and some with no airflow.
- **Findings:** the moment method with a lognormal assumption is a valid
  approach; zero-flow smoke particles were 2 to 3 times larger than at 8
  cm/s; the prototype 2-moment detector (MPASS) was very encouraging.
- **Caveat:** the report gives 66 test points while its excerpted paper
  says over 100 samples were tested; both are kept.
- Smoke / aerosol class: not comparable with any flame measurement.
