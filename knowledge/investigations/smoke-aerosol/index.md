# Smoke aerosol

* [SAME: Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment](same.md) - Size distributions of smoke from overheated (not burning) spacecraft materials, measured in the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox in 2007, from the SAME final report.
* [SAME-R: Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment Re-flight](same-r.md) - The 2010 re-flight of SAME — new material (Pyrell), higher sample temperatures and no-flow runs, a new two-moment detector, and ground tests that check the moment method — from the SAMER final report.
