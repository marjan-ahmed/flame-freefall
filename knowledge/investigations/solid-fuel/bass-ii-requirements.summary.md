The plan behind BASS-II: its requirements, success criteria and sample
manifest, from briefing charts. It says what BASS-II set out to do, not what
it found; for results, read the BASS-II entry.

- **Objectives:** find the worst case for material flammability in
  spacecraft, compare it with Earth's upward-burning screening test, and
  learn how quickly a fire in space grows and whether it reaches a finite
  size.
- **Samples:** 20 for each of the 5 PIs, including PMMA films, slabs, rods,
  a 4 cm sphere, SIBAL and NOMEX fabric.
- **Flow velocity was to be overlaid on the video**, at 4 Hz, with ±10%
  accuracy. That is why the PSI table has only control-panel readings, not
  velocities in cm/s.
- **Complete success:** all PI samples (100) tested at the desired
  conditions.
- **Open question:** the requirements count 5 PIs, while the BASS-II summary
  report has six per-PI test matrices.
