BASS, the first Burning and Suppression of Solids campaign, burned solid
samples in a small wind tunnel in the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox, with
airflow up to 40 cm/s. It tested the hypothesis that materials in
microgravity, with adequate ventilation, burn as well as or better than on
Earth.

- **Scale:** 41 samples, 115 burns: thin and thick flat samples, spheres and
  candles.
- **Thin fuel:** in opposed flow, spread was fastest at an intermediate flow
  speed; in concurrent flow it rose linearly with flow, and quenching
  extinction came around 1 cm/s.
- **Fire-retarded materials:** Ultem and Nomex HT90-40 did not burn at 21% to
  22% O2; a Nomex-III sample did burn at 22% O2.
- **Also burned:** PMMA spheres and candles at 17% oxygen, at flows down to
  less than 1 cm/s.
- **Data in PSI:** images and video in two datasets, BASS_2012 and BASS_2013.
- The results source is a conference poster, not a technical report.
