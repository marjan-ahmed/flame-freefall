NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) data repository was opened in 2015
and holds most of the original data from reduced-gravity experiments, open to
anyone. This record uses three kinds of PSI file:

- **Experimental tables**, catalogued in "PSI experimental tables".
- **Summary and matrix workbooks**, catalogued in "PSI summary and matrix
  workbooks".
- **Reports**, which are the sources of the investigation documents.

One workbook is deliberately never opened: it contains account credentials.
