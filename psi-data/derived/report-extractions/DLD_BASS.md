

PSI Data Layout Document - BASS (Burning and Suppression of Solids) Investigation


BASS (Burning and Suppression of Solids) examines the burning and extinction characteristics of a wide
variety of fuel samples in microgravity. The BASS experiment will guide strategies for extinguishing
accidental fires in microgravity. BASS results contribute to the combustion computational models used
in the design of fire detection and suppression systems in microgravity and on Earth. The BASS data
available in PSI is organized into 2 datasets by the year the tests were conducted:


- BASS_2012

- BASS_2013


These Experiment Data records are located on the "Data" tab within the BASS investigation in PSI. When
an experiment data record is selected, the "Scientific Data and Information" tab is opened. For
BASS_2012, on the "Scientific Data and Information" tab, "Images" are organized by date formatted
yyyy_mm_dd. There are 21 image galleries for BASS_2012. For BASS_2013, on the "Scientific Data and
Information" tab, "Audio/Video Files" and "Images" are both organized by date formatted yyyy_mm_dd.
There are 12 audio/video collections and 15 image galleries for BASS_2013.


Other relevant data and information about the BASS investigation can be located throughout the other
tabs in the BASS record.
