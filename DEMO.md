1. **Question (15 seconds).** ?Before choosing a material for an exploration cabin,
   which experiments actually inform the proposed atmosphere?? Open the home page.
2. **Test the assumption (25 seconds).** Read the Lunar table's default row, then
   the tighter-pressure row. Point out that retrieval counts change with the window;
   the experiment has not changed. Do not describe matches as certified safe materials.
3. **Reveal the overlooked evidence (25 seconds).** Open the BRE cell. Inspect
   report test `19070D1`, its original atmosphere cells, steady-flame observation,
   source caption, preliminary status and conflicting heat-of-combustion values.
   Explain that a gaseous emulator informs condensed-fuel research without being
   a direct test of a real solid sample.
4. **Inspect the boundary (20 seconds).** Return to the direct-solid column and
   widen oxygen tolerance. Follow the nearest SAFFIRE record to its source. A nearby
   atmosphere is a useful comparison candidate, not validated lunar-gravity behavior.
5. **Leave with usable evidence (20 seconds).** Download the evidence JSON. Show
   query, tolerances, dataset hash, run IDs and per-outcome source indexes. A colleague
   can inspect how the result was produced without trusting a model's prose.
6. **Close (15 seconds).** ?The next experiment starts with a reproducible account
   of the evidence we have, the conditions we do not know, and the comparisons we
   cannot yet defend.? State that expert validation and competition eligibility remain open.

## Replacement pitch storyline

- Problem: fragmented experimental evidence makes unsupported comparisons easy.
- User: researcher selecting experiments for an exploration-atmosphere study.
- Contribution: source-preserving normalization plus reproducible, sensitivity-aware retrieval.
- Demonstration: solid samples, indirect BRE evidence and other gaseous experiments
  remain distinct; search-window changes are visible.
- Validation: actual test report, source trace and known failures; no ?hallucination impossible? claim.
- Next validation: a combustion specialist reproduces one investigation and assesses
  whether the exported bundle improves experiment selection. No adoption is claimed.

Avoid a fixed count slide. Capture the final built table with its windows and record
scope visible. Keep source links and eligibility status on the final slide.
